# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, engineers, investors, collaborators, press, and curious job seekers who want a fast, credible understanding of Agnay Srivastava, what he is building, and how he thinks.

## Product Purpose

A durable personal home for Agnay after becoming a Y Combinator founder: introduce the person, make Tsenta the clear center of gravity, preserve a selective record of earlier work, and provide a first-class place to publish personal writing. Success means a visitor understands the founder, the company, and the point of view in under a minute, then has an obvious path to read more or get in touch.

## Brand Personality

Candid, restless, precise. Technically serious without posturing, ambitious without turning YC into the personality, and playful enough to sound like the person behind the work.

The core thesis is personal leverage: AI should make one person far more capable, useful software should save more than it costs, and the best product ideas begin with a problem Agnay or someone close to him has actually felt.

## Anti-references

- The old cosmic 3D portfolio, technology icon clouds, animated skill cards, and tutorial-template visuals.
- Generic AI founder sites: black backgrounds, purple gradients, glass panels, glowing orbs, and vague claims about changing the world.
- Default shadcn dashboards disguised as portfolios.
- Resume dumps, fake testimonials, vanity metrics, and unverified impact claims.
- Editorial-magazine affectations that make a builder look like a fashion publication.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the work now: Tsenta is the main story, earlier projects are supporting evidence.
- Sound lived-in: use concrete problems, decisions, and outcomes instead of capability lists.
- Treat work history as plot, not credentials: keep the useful rejections, unexpected lessons, and decisions that changed what came next.
- Let the page move quickly: strong hierarchy, short paths, and no interaction tax.
- Earn every claim: prefer first-party links and verified public facts.
- Keep publishing friction low: writing lives in versioned MDX with no CMS until editing in Git becomes painful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, useful alternative text, responsive reading widths, and a complete reduced-motion experience. Core content must remain available without client-side JavaScript.
